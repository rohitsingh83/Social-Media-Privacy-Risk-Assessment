HOW THE FIELD WORKS

Privacy, with the lights on.

A transparent, rule-based educational framework. It shows its assumptions, keeps the user in control, and treats uncertainty as a reason to review—not as proof of danger.

01⌁

Privacy ≠ security

Privacy controls how information is exposed and used. Security protects accounts and systems from unauthorized access. A strong password can coexist with a very public profile.

ExampleMFA ON + contact details PUBLIC = better account security, unchanged information exposure.
02◌

Footprint, in context

Active footprint is content you choose to share. Passive footprint can be generated by services depending on their context. This tool evaluates only self-reported practices.

Look backOld posts, comments, photos, unused accounts, and profile history can outlive your intended audience.
03↗

Small clues, big context

Public work, school, interests, travel, family references, or events may make deceptive contact seem more believable. That is a reason for awareness—not a reason to profile anyone.

Defensive habitPause. Verify through a trusted channel. Never share sign-in codes.
04✳

Scores are explainable

Ten category scores are weighted to 0–100. LOW 0–20, MODERATE 21–40, HIGH 41–70, CRITICAL 71–100. Higher means more assessed exposure.

Not a verdictWeights and thresholds are educational assumptions—not a professional risk certification.
