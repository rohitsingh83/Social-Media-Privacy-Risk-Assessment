# Privacy Self-Assessment Questionnaire

> These questions assess settings and habits only. Do not enter any actual phone number, email, full birthday, password, address, precise location, account handle, photo, or private-message content. “Not sure” is a valid response; review the setting directly in the platform later.

## Profile visibility — 10% weight

How much of your profile and social graph can be discovered?

01. **How visible is your main profile to people you do not know?**
   - Choose the broadest audience that can see profile details. Visibility is not automatically unsafe; it changes exposure.
   - Choices: Public, Followers / friends, Private, Not sure

02. **Can search engines show your profile or posts?**
   - Think about the platform's search-indexing control, not a search for your own name.
   - Choices: Yes, No, Sometimes, Not sure

03. **Can people find your profile using contact details they already know?**
   - Do not enter a phone number or email. Answer only about the discoverability setting.
   - Choices: Yes, No, Sometimes, Not sure

04. **Can anyone view your followers or following list?**
   - A visible social graph can reveal interests, affiliations, or relationships.
   - Choices: Yes, No, Sometimes, Not sure

05. **Is your activity status or recent activity visible to a broad audience?**
   - Examples include online status, last active, or public likes and follows.
   - Choices: Yes, No, Sometimes, Not sure
